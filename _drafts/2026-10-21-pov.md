I had a terrible realisation about the activities organised by the private school I attended as a child.

I know Jordanians are clever, but the bureaucracy is a bit of a mess and the society is much less defined than it is in abroad. I have been taught this is something to look down upon, and that the public system is one of chaos as that my private school experienced lived on a pedestal. It is true my access to opportunities is far greater given my start in life, but there should be no conflation between accesss, ability, and nurturing towards success or innovation. Likewise, I have recently been dreading the move back home. In part this is due to my ASD, which makes dealing with the fuzzy definedness of processes and strong social requirements difficult. In other part, it is perhaps a form of internalised racism as is extremely common in my country and particularly in my social bubble. I pointed out the dangers of what I realised o my mother, and we wondered why so many more jordanian children per capita are clever, vs the same ratio in canadians. Perhaps it is a response to this unpredictable yet workable environment that keeps people creative and forces constant perception and quit witedness. I am excited to go back and train these skills and learn from my people. Peace out.
